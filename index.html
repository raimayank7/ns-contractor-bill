Free running account (RA) bill maker for contractors

Bill each stage of a contract the way engineers check it: every BOQ item with its rate, the quantity done before, in this bill and up to date, the GST on this bill, and the deductions the client will make. Next month, one tap carries the quantities forward.

How to make a RA bill here

  1. Type your firm and the client. Your name, address, GSTIN and bank details once; then the client, the name of work, site and work order number.
  2. Add the BOQ items. Item number, description, unit, rate and BOQ quantity, as in your work order. Saved items come back with unit and rate.
  3. Enter quantities. Previous quantity (up to the last bill) and the quantity done in this bill. The up-to-date quantity is worked out, with a warning if it goes above the BOQ.
  4. Set GST and deductions. GST on this bill (18% in most cases), retention or security deposit, income-tax TDS, GST TDS for government clients, and any advance or material recovery.
  5. Save and print. The bill prints on A4 with the three quantity and three amount columns, the bill amount, every deduction and the net payable, and a place for the engineer’s and your signature.
  6. Next month, press "Next RA bill". Open the last bill from Saved RA bills: this bill’s quantities move into "previous", the RA number goes up by one, and you type only the new quantities.

What it does for you

  • Three-column measurement: previous, this bill and up to date, in quantity and amount, for every BOQ item.
  • BOQ progress: the percentage of the contract value done, and a mark on items above the BOQ quantity.
  • Works contract GST: CGST and SGST or IGST on the value of this bill.
  • All the usual deductions: retention, income-tax TDS on the value without GST, GST TDS 2%, advance and material recovery, other deductions with a note.
  • Carry forward: the next RA bill starts from this one’s up-to-date quantities.
  • In your books: each bill’s value shows as income from the client in the free Nivesh Sahayak App.

Your data stays with you

Everything you type is kept in this browser on this device. There is no account and nothing is uploaded to us. Your businesses, customers, suppliers and items are shared with the other Nivesh Sahayak document tools through my.niveshsahayak.in, so details you saved while making an invoice or a quotation are already here as suggestions.

Keep a backup. If the browser's data is cleared, saved work in it is gone. Open My data (top of the page) and press Save a backup file now and then. One file holds every document, customer and item, and restores them on any other computer or phone.

Questions people ask

Is this RA bill maker free?

Yes. Making, saving, printing and sharing RA bills is free, with no login and no limit.

Does it show previous, this bill and up-to-date quantities?

Yes, in quantity and amount for every BOQ item, with totals. See RA bill format.

How is GST worked out?

On the value of work in this bill, at 18% in most cases, as CGST + SGST or IGST. See GST on works contract.

Can I show retention and TDS?

Yes: retention, income-tax TDS on the value without GST, GST TDS 2%, advance and material recovery and other deductions. See deductions.

How do I make the next bill?

Open the last bill and press "Next RA bill": quantities carry forward and the RA number goes up. See next and final bill.

Will it warn me above the BOQ?

Yes. Items whose up-to-date quantity is above the BOQ quantity are marked, and the maker asks before saving.

Can I print it for the engineer?

Yes, on A4 with the measurement columns, deductions, net payable and signature places for the engineer-in-charge and you.

Where is my data kept?

In this browser on this device, shared with the other Nivesh Sahayak tools through my.niveshsahayak.in. Nothing is sent to us. Save a backup file regularly.
